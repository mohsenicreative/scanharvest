Megascans Claimer

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · applies to version 1.1.0 and later

Short version: Megascans Claimer does not collect, sell, share or transmit any personal data. Everything it stores stays in your own browser.

What the extension does

Megascans Claimer adds free Quixel Megascans listings on fab.com to your Fab library by pressing Fab's own "Add to My Library" button in a tab where you are already signed in. It has a single purpose and only runs on https://www.fab.com/*.

Data we collect

None. The developer operates no servers, analytics, tracking pixels, crash reporting or advertising. The extension contains no remote code.

Data stored on your device

To let you pause and resume, the extension keeps the following in chrome.storage.local on your computer. It never leaves your browser and is removed when you click "Start over" or uninstall the extension.

ItemWhy
Counters (added, already owned, need retry, elapsed time) and the current status messageShown in the popup and progress panel
Addresses of Fab listings already processed, and titles of listings that need a retryTo skip finished items and retry failed ones
Your chosen interface languageTo show the interface in that language

Your Fab and Epic Games account

The extension never reads, stores or sends passwords, cookies, tokens or account details. Requests to Fab are the same ones Fab's own page makes when you click its buttons, sent directly from your browser to Fab. Your use of Fab is governed by Epic Games' own terms and privacy policy.
